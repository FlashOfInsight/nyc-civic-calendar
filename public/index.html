We are moving to a new URL

Following Vercel's recent security incident, we are proactively migrating NYC Civic Calendar off Vercel to Cloudflare. Our data was not affected — we are switching hosts out of an abundance of caution.

The new home is nycciviccalendar.com. Please re-subscribe your calendar feed there before June 30, 2026. Subscriptions to the old URL will stop updating after that date.

Important: remove your existing subscription first, then add the new one. Adding the new feed without removing the old will cause duplicate events in your calendar.

How it works

  1. Select the meetings you want to track below
  2. Copy your personalized calendar URL
  3. Add it to your calendar app (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar)
  4. Your calendar will automatically update with new meetings

Your Calendar URL

Add this URL as a calendar subscription in your calendar app

Google Calendar
  1. Open Google Calendar
  2. Click the + next to "Other calendars"
  3. Select "From URL"
  4. Paste your calendar URL
  5. Click "Add calendar"
Apple Calendar
  1. Open Calendar app
  2. Go to File → New Calendar Subscription
  3. Paste your calendar URL
  4. Click Subscribe
Outlook
  1. Open Outlook Calendar
  2. Click "Add calendar" → "Subscribe from web"
  3. Paste your calendar URL
  4. Click "Import"
Note: Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule (usually every 12-24 hours). If you change your selections and want to see updates immediately, you'll need to remove the calendar subscription and re-add it with your new URL.
